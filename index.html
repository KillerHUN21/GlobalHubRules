📜 Server Rules

Please read and follow these rules while participating in Global Hub.

1. 🤝 Respect

Treat every member with respect, regardless of nationality, age, role or opinion. Harassment, personal attacks, humiliation and deliberate provocation are not allowed. Healthy discussions are welcome; personal arguments are not.

2. 🚫 No Hate

Hate speech, discrimination or offensive content targeting individuals or groups is prohibited. Racist, homophobic, antisemitic or otherwise hateful content is not allowed. Calling something a joke does not exempt it from the rules.

3. 💬 Channel Usage

Use channels according to their intended purpose. Do not flood or spam channels. Moderators may move or remove content posted in the wrong channel.

4. 📢 Advertising

Advertising other servers, communities, services or projects requires permission. Unsolicited advertising through DMs or mass invitations is prohibited. Share your own projects only in designated channels.

5. 🔗 Links & Files

Malicious, harmful or misleading files and links are prohibited. Moderators may remove suspicious links. Do not share other people's personal information without permission.

6. 🔞 Inappropriate Content

Pornographic, sexually explicit or otherwise inappropriate content is prohibited. Shock content and excessively violent material are not allowed. Attempts to bypass these rules are also considered violations.

7. 🛡️ Privacy

Do not share personal information about yourself or others. Doxxing, threatening exposure or other misuse of personal information is prohibited. Do not share private messages without the consent of the people involved.

8. 🎙️ Voice Chat

Do not intentionally disrupt other users. Mic-spamming, excessive noise and soundboard spam are prohibited. Recording or streaming other users should only be done with appropriate consent.

9. 🤖 Bots & Exploits

Exploiting bots, Discord features or other vulnerabilities is prohibited. If you discover a vulnerability or bug, report it to the Staff team. Deliberately disrupting other users or the server is prohibited.

10. 👮 Staff Decisions

The Staff team is responsible for enforcing the rules and maintaining a safe community. Do not argue about moderation decisions in public channels. If you disagree with a decision, respectfully appeal through the appropriate ticket or moderation channel. Staff members are not above the rules.

11. ⚖️ Punishments

Depending on the severity of a violation, the following actions may be taken: ⚠️ Warning 🔇 Timeout / Mute 🚫 Kick 🔨 Ban Staff will determine the appropriate punishment based on the severity and history of the violation. Severe violations may result in an immediate ban without prior warning. Evading punishments may result in additional action.

12. 🌐 Common Sense

Not every possible situation can be listed in the rules, so use common sense. Staff may take action against behaviour that clearly harms, disrupts or threatens the community, even if the exact situation is not explicitly listed. Global Hub aims to be a friendly, international and active community.

🌍 Welcome to Global Hub!

Respect the community and help us build a friendly and welcoming server.
